This is not a bad website

Your service pages are better than both competitors we measured, your structured data is correct, and you have more click-to-call links than either of them. Somebody built this properly.

What is wrong with it is a short list, and most of it is free to fix. That is why we are not recommending you spend money on traffic yet.

Finding 1 — There Is Template Placeholder Text On Your Homepage
SERVED FROM lansingmechanicaltx.com, 30 SEPTEMBER 2026 "(940) 229-0715 Serving Graham, TX and Surrounding Areas , Business Tagline Lorem Ipsum Dolor (940) 229-0715" "Lorem Ipsum Dolor" is dummy Latin that website builders use to fill a space before the real words are written. It is sitting in your header, between two copies of your phone number, on the first thing anybody sees.
What this costs you

Anybody who notices it learns that nobody has looked at this website in a long time. For a contractor whose whole pitch is care and workmanship, that is the wrong thing to be signalling in the header.

It takes about two minutes to fix and it does not need us. Whoever has access to the site editor can replace it with a real tagline today. We would rather tell you than save it for a pitch.

Where the real tagline should come from

You already have the material. Emergency service available, guaranteed satisfaction, one year install labour warranty, and a Texas licence number on the page. Any one of those is a stronger line than a tagline.

Finding 2 — Nobody Can Contact You In Writing
FORMS FOUND, ACROSS ALL EIGHT PAGES / 0 /contact 0 a contact page with no form on it /hvac-services 0 /refrigeration-services 0 /commercial-equipment 0 /about 0 /reviews 0 /careers 0 Phone links: 6. Email links: 2.
For a lot of your work this genuinely does not matter

Emergency refrigeration does not get booked through a form. Somebody with a walk-in going warm picks up the phone, and your six click-to-call links serve that properly.

It matters for the other kind of enquiry. A commercial equipment quote, a planned install, a restaurant manager comparing contractors at nine at night when you are closed. Your hours are Monday to Friday eight to five and you are shut at weekends. Everything that arrives outside those hours has nowhere to land.

And it matters specifically for what you asked about

You told Paige you only want to run something in the slow months. A campaign pointed at a site with no form has one way to convert, and it is a phone call during business hours.

That is the single biggest reason a previous lead generation company could have produced nothing. We have not seen what they ran and we are not going to guess. But traffic to a site that cannot take a written enquiry loses everybody who was not ready to ring.

Finding 3 — Your Analytics Is Partly Dead And You Cannot Tell What Works
TAGS FOUND ON THE HOMEPAGE Google Tag Manager GTM-W487CGFD present gtag() present Meta pixel fbq( found present Classic Analytics _gaq found 9 times RETIRED PLATFORM Google Ads conversion AW- tag NOT FOUND So: something is counting visits. Nothing is counting jobs.
The classic Analytics code stopped collecting data years ago

The _gaq code on your site belongs to a version of Google Analytics that was switched off. It is still loading on every page and it is recording nothing. It is a leftover.

The risk is not the dead code itself, it is what it implies. If anybody has told you they were watching your analytics, it is worth asking which property they were looking at.

The Meta pixel is a genuine asset and neither competitor has one

You are the only firm of the three with a Facebook pixel installed. Air Smart has none. Graham Heating and Air has none.

If you ever do run something seasonal, that pixel is the difference between advertising to strangers and advertising to people who have already been on your site. It is already there and already collecting.

Finding 4 — The Reviews Page Is 136 Words
WORDS PER PAGE /hvac-services 643 / 640 /commercial-equipment 626 /refrigeration-services 616 /careers 246 /about 225 /contact 164 /reviews 136 Review or rating structured data: none found
Your four service pages are strong and your four supporting pages are thin

Whoever wrote the service pages did a proper job. Six hundred words each on HVAC, refrigeration and commercial equipment is more than either competitor puts on their homepage.

Then the pages that build trust were left almost empty. A reviews page with 136 words on it does less for you than no reviews page, because somebody clicks it expecting proof and finds a heading.

For commercial work that gap matters most. A restaurant or a grocery choosing a refrigeration contractor checks who else trusted you before they ring.

What We Would Do, In Order
  • Replace the Lorem Ipsum today. Free, two minutes, and it does not need us.
  • Put a form on the contact page and the three service pages. Name, number, what has gone wrong, whether it is residential or commercial. That alone gives every out-of-hours enquiry somewhere to go.
  • Remove the dead classic Analytics and install conversion tracking properly. Until that exists, nobody can honestly tell you whether anything worked, including us.
  • Fill out the reviews page and add review structured data, so the proof shows up in search rather than only on a page nobody visits.
  • Then, and only then, decide about the slow months. With a form and tracking in place, a seasonal campaign can be measured. Without them it cannot, which is how the last one went.
On the no-contract point, since that is what interested you

Nothing above requires a contract and nothing recurring is being proposed in this document. The site work is a one-off. If you later want something run in your slow months, that is a separate decision you make with a month of your own numbers in front of you.

Better service pages than both competitors, and a homepage that still says Lorem Ipsum.

The gap between those two facts is the whole of this document.
