Week One Is Measurement. Nothing Is Spent Until It Is In.

Lansing Mechanical LLC · prepared for Kristina · AG1 Consulting

You told Paige a lead generation company did nothing for you, so we have not written you a lead generation pitch.

All three documents are about the site you already own - what it does well, the one thing on it you will want to change today, and where it cannot do the job.

Three documents • Start with the audit • Concept site is a proposal, not a live site
Exactly what happens, week by week

You said you would rather put money into your guys than into something that may or may not return. ⭐ The fairest answer to that is not a promise, it is a sequence with dates on it, so you can tell at any point whether we are doing what we said.

Week 1 · Measurement, before anything is spent
  • Conversion tracking installed on the form and on every phone link
  • The Google tag added, and the Meta pixel you already own finally wired up
  • Enquiry form put on every page, not only the contact page
  • The placeholder text removed from your homepage
  • Local business markup added: your address, hours and service area, in a form Google reads

🔴 No advertising runs in week one. ⭐ Spending before the measurement is installed is how the last two attempts became unarguable, and we are not repeating it.

Week 2 · The pages that sell winter work
  • Heating page built, because that is what people need in December
  • Maintenance special page built, with the July priority promise at the top
  • Your Google reviews pulled onto your own website, where they currently are not

⚠ On option A these pages are not included. The campaigns then run to pages we host instead, which works, but you do not keep the ranking benefit.

Week 3 · The first campaign goes live
  • Meta, heating check and maintenance plan, with the qualifying questions in front of the form
  • Your own name on Google search, small, so nobody else buys it
  • Podium connected, so a lead that arrives while you are on a roof still gets an answer
  • ⭐ We call the first ten leads ourselves, with you listening if you want. Not to sell. To find out whether they are any good, which is the only question you actually asked
Week 4 · The first numbers you have ever had
  • A report showing where every enquiry came from, which page, which campaign, what it cost
  • A call about what to kill and what to feed
  • ⚠⚠ It will be a small month and we would rather say so now. December is your quiet season, the account is new, and anybody promising you a big first month is guessing or lying
🔴🔴 What week four is actually for

⭐⭐ It is not for proving the advertising worked. It is for proving the measurement works.

Sixteen years in, you still cannot say which half of your marketing produces work. Neither can either of your competitors, which we checked. By the end of month one you can, and that is true whether the first campaign does well or badly.

⚠ That is the thing you are actually buying in month one. The jobs come after, in a season that has not started yet.

⚠ Nothing on this page promises a number of leads, jobs or dollars. It is a list of what we will do and when, so that you can hold us to the sequence rather than to a forecast. ⭐ If a week slips, you will be told in that week rather than at the end of the month.
