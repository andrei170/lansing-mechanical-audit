🔴🔴 Your question first, because everything else depends on the answer

You asked: "tell me how you actually guarantee that it's not bogus leads."

🔴 We cannot guarantee that, and anyone who tells you otherwise is guessing.

⭐⭐ But we are not selling you leads, and that is the whole difference. The pay-per-lead company sixteen years ago sold you names. Trane sends you names. You own none of it, so when the names are poor you have no way to see why and no way to change it.

⭐ Here, the ad account is yours, the tracking is yours and the data is yours. The tracking is the thing that tells you which leads are bogus, which page they came from and what they searched. ⚠ You currently have none of that, which is exactly why the last two attempts could never be diagnosed, only abandoned.

🔴🔴 The timing, and it is the reason we are having this conversation in October rather than March

You told us: ten calls a day in summer, one or two in winter.

⭐⭐ The accepted practice in this trade is to start six to eight weeks before a season, not during it. For winter heating that window is early October. We are inside it right now.

⚠⚠ We have to be straight with you about one thing we got wrong. We initially thought commercial refrigeration would carry you through winter. Checking it properly, Texas refrigeration failures peak May to August too, when ambient heat pushes compressors past their design capacity. So refrigeration is a summer business as well, and building that page now is about ranking before May, not about January revenue.

🔴 So the honest winter plan is heating work and maintenance plans, and the honest statement about January is that no amount of advertising creates demand for air conditioning in a Texas winter. ⭐ Which is why the monthly fee is not flat.
