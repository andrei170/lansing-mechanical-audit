🔴🔴 The timing, and why this is a conversation for October

You told us: ten calls a day in summer, and one or two a day from October to January.

⭐⭐ Your quiet season is not coming. It has started. Your four technicians and your husband are paid through October, November, December and January whether the phone rings or not. Every week this waits is another week of capacity you have already bought sitting idle.

⚠⚠ One thing we got wrong and want to correct. We first thought commercial refrigeration could carry you through winter. Checked properly, Texas refrigeration failures peak May to August, when the heat pushes compressors past their design capacity, so it is a summer business too. We build that page now so it is ranking before May, not for January revenue.

🔴 And no amount of advertising creates demand for air conditioning in a Texas winter. The winter work is service calls: heating checks, tune-ups and maintenance plans, which is exactly the kind of call your idle technicians can take.

⚠⚠ About Hibu, since you were kind enough to send us the link

You told us people are "all the time" trying to sell you a change of website hosting. ⭐⭐ So we are not going to be another one. You do not have to leave Hibu, switch anything or move anything to work with us. The campaigns run to pages we host, and your Hibu site stays exactly where it is.

🔴 What we would suggest instead is four questions to ask Hibu yourself. They come from the patterns that show up most often in Hibu's own public reviews. We are not saying any of them apply to you. You would know, and they would tell you.

  • "If I stop paying, do I keep my website?" ⚠ The single most common complaint in their public reviews is businesses losing their site, content and domain setup when they leave, and being told the site belonged to Hibu
  • "Whose name is my domain registered in?" If it is not yours, your web address is not fully yours either
  • "When does my contract end, and what does cancelling involve?" Several public reviews describe being billed for months after cancelling
  • "Am I paying you for anything beyond the website?" ⭐⭐ This is the one that matters most. Hibu also sells ads, SEO and listings. If you are paying for any of those, they cannot currently be measured, because your site has no conversion tracking. That is the same problem you described with Trane's leads, from a different supplier
🔴 One thing on your current site that is not a matter of opinion

The words "Business Tagline Lorem Ipsum Dolor" appear on every page of your live website: the homepage, About and Contact.

⚠ "Lorem ipsum" is placeholder text that website builders put in a template, meant to be replaced before a site goes live. It is in white italic, in the row near the bottom of each page, which is why it is easy to miss on your own screen and still read by Google and by anyone who looks closely.

⭐⭐ We are not raising it to criticise Hibu. We are raising it because you are paying someone to look after that site, and it has been there long enough to be on every page. ⚠ It is a two-minute fix, and it is worth asking why it was not caught.
