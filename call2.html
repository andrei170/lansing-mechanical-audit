You Said It Was Too Much. You Were Right.

Prepared for Kristina Lansing · for our call on Monday 19 October, 2pm Central · AG1 Consulting

Our call on 7 October, and our phone call after it, in one place. What you told me, what you pushed back on, and what I changed because of it.

⭐⭐ What you told me on 7 October
  • You do every job in the office: “Marketing, billing, collections, deposits, taxes. Communication.”
  • You tried ServiceTitan and Housecall Pro, and went back. “My guys are more not okay with change and learning something new.” ServiceTitan “tracked everything” but “it was just too much for us.”
  • After a year back on your old program, the team is finally “in a groove”, and you do not want to lose that
  • You bill from QuickBooks Online: the schedule program makes the invoice, and you review and send it
  • You stopped the annual maintenance plan because the billing was extra work and the tickets came out lower. You still keep customers on a schedule and message them through Podium
  • Your quiet season is October to February, and “February is when we get real cold.” From March you are slammed
  • “I’m visual”: you wanted it in writing, so you can look at it
⚠ What you pushed back on
  • “Why pay more during my busy season?” You were right: a small town is not going to ramp up the way a city does. The monthly now runs November to February only, and is paused from March
  • “I wouldn’t just cancel from March to October”, but you did not want to pay for months you do not need. So a pause keeps everything: the campaigns, the data and the pages stay put, ready to switch back on
  • On our phone call after that: it was too much, and you did not need everything. That was fair, and it is the reason for the new setup below
  • The domain: you did not want to lose your Google listing by starting on a new address. Nothing has to move before we start
⭐⭐ What I changed
  • Less than half the price, paid once: $1,100 or $1,500, instead of $3,000 plus a monthly fee
  • Built around your office: one inbox for every call, text, Facebook message and form, a text back to every missed call, reminders on open quotes, and your maintenance reminders sending themselves
  • Your techs change nothing. They stay on the program they know. This sits in front of it, for you
  • No monthly fee unless you want one. The winter ads are optional, November to February only
