Three things this table says immediately

Nobody in Graham is running Google Ads conversion tracking. Not one of the three has an AW- tag. The whole local field is spending on faith or not spending at all.

You are the only one with a Meta pixel. Neither competitor could retarget a visitor if they wanted to.

Graham Heating and Air is the only one of the three who can take a written enquiry. That is the one column where somebody is ahead of you and it is the cheapest to close.

Finding 1 — Graham Heating & Air Publishes Nearly Twice What You Do
HOMEPAGE DEPTH Graham Heating & Air 1,165 words · 4 H2 · a working enquiry form Lansing Mechanical 640 words · 4 H2 · no form Air Smart 570 words · 1 H2 · no form
But look at where your words actually are

Your homepage is 640 words and theirs is 1,165. On the homepage they are ahead.

Your service pages are the reverse. HVAC 643, refrigeration 616, commercial equipment 626. Each one of those is longer than Air Smart's entire homepage, and close to Graham Heating's.

So you are not short of content. It is distributed differently. They put the weight on the front door; you put it behind three specific doors. Yours is arguably the better structure for search, and the worse one for a visitor who lands on the homepage and stops there.

Finding 2 — The One Column Where Somebody Beats You Costs Almost Nothing To Close
WHO CAN TAKE AN ENQUIRY WITHOUT THE PHONE RINGING Graham Heating & Air yes -- 1 form Lansing Mechanical no Air Smart no YOUR OPENING HOURS Mon-Fri 8:00am - 5:00pm. Sat-Sun closed. Emergency service available.
Two of the three of you only exist between eight and five

Your emergency line covers the genuine emergencies and it should. A walk-in failing on a Saturday is a phone call, not a form, and your six click-to-call links handle that better than either competitor.

The planned work is different. A commercial equipment replacement, a scheduled install, a facilities manager gathering three quotes. That person is often looking in the evening, and if they land on your site or Air Smart's there is nothing to do except remember to ring tomorrow.

At Graham Heating they can type it and go to bed. That is the entire advantage, and it is one form.

Finding 3 — Nobody Is Measuring Whether Any Of This Produces Work
GOOGLE ADS CONVERSION TAG (AW-) Lansing Mechanical none Graham Heating & Air none Air Smart none Three firms. Zero conversion tracking. Including you. WHAT EACH OF YOU DOES HAVE Lansing GTM + gtag + Meta pixel + retired classic Analytics Graham Heating GTM Air Smart GA4
This is the part that matters for the slow months

You told Paige a lead generation company produced nothing. With no conversion tag anywhere on this site, there would have been no way to prove it either way at the time, and there still isn't.

That is worth fixing before anything is spent, not after. It is also the cheapest item on the list and it is not a monthly cost.

And the whole of Graham is in the same position, so this is available to you before it occurs to either of them.

Finding 4 — You Are Hiring, And So Is The Problem
ON YOUR OWN HOMEPAGE, TODAY "Now Hiring Technicians - Apply on Careers Page" /careers 246 words
We are flagging this because it changes what is worth buying

You are advertising for technicians on your own front page. If the constraint on the business right now is people rather than work, then more enquiries is not automatically the right purchase, and we would rather say so than sell you the opposite.

Worth asking on the call: in your slow months, is the slow part the phone or the schedule? If the phone still rings and you simply cannot staff the jobs, the answer is not a campaign.

Your careers page is 246 words, which is the same problem as the reviews page in a different costume. A technician deciding where to apply reads that page the way a customer reads the reviews one.

What The Field Tells Us To Do
  • Put a form on the site and close the only column you are behind in. One item, and it takes you level with the strongest of the three.
  • Install conversion tracking and clear out the dead Analytics. Nobody in Graham is doing this, and without it nothing that follows can be judged.
  • Move some weight onto the homepage. Not because 640 words is bad, but because somebody who lands there and leaves never sees the three good pages behind it.
  • Use the pixel you already have. You are the only firm here who could retarget, and it is already installed and already collecting.
  • Settle the staffing question before any spend. If the bottleneck is technicians, the careers page is the higher-value thing to fix first, and we would build that instead.

One form is the entire gap between you and the best-built site in Graham.

Everything else in this comparison, you already lead on or nobody is doing.
