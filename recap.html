Call recap · Lansing Mechanical LLC · Graham, Texas

Both Our Calls, In One Page.

Prepared for Kristina Lansing · 15 October 2026 · AG1 Consulting

Our two calls, on 6 and 7 October, in one place, so you and your husband can read it before Friday instead of rewatching. The recordings are still there if you want them.

⭐⭐ What you told me
  • The goal is consistency through the slow season, with a team you can train and keep, not more chaos in summer
  • Summer is full: about 10 calls a day, roughly 50 service calls a week
  • October to February is quiet: “we may only have one or two” a day, and February is when it gets really cold
  • In winter you could easily handle five to ten a day
  • About two installs a week all year, and an average ticket of about $500
  • Your team: Tyler, Jimmy, Kane and Blake, plus your husband and you. 16 years trading, Trane and Mitsubishi
  • No commercial work for now. You are keeping the school district, the county jail and your existing restaurant customer
  • You already contact your maintenance customers through Podium every autumn
  • You cover about 30 miles around Graham, out to Jacksboro and Breckenridge, and Possum Kingdom Lake is where you most want to reach
  • You would rather put money into your guys than into something that may or may not return
What you asked
  • “How do you guarantee the leads aren’t bogus?” I won’t guarantee it, because that is what the last company said. Instead, you listen in while we ring the first ten enquiries, so you judge them yourself
  • Whose name is the domain in? That is a question for Hibu. It renews on 19 December 2026, which is the natural point to move it. Nothing has to wait for it
The plan we landed on
  • Ads run October to February, when you need the work, and everything pauses March to September while you are full. Nothing is deleted
  • Three offers for the quiet season: a maintenance special (what you already do through Podium, taken to people who are not customers yet), a heating check and tune-up, and mini splits for garages, additions and home offices
  • Enquiries come in through a short form and get a call back within 5 minutes
  • Every call and form is tracked to where it came from, and you get a monthly report
⚠ Still open
  • What Hibu said about the domain and your agreement
  • Which of the two options suits you, and how you would like to pay for the build
  • To start: access to your Google Business Profile, your Facebook page and ad account, and Podium. You add us as a user, so no passwords are needed
