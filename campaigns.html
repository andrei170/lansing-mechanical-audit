Which platform, when, and what to put on it
🔴🔴 You answered the platform question yourself on our call

Your words about a summer customer: "if it's 110, and you go home from work, and you walk in your house, and your house is 95 degrees, you're not getting on the internet and going through my web page... you're like, can you get here right away? I need you who can be here the fastest. And so it's more of like, just searching air conditioner, Graham Texas."

⭐⭐ That is a precise description of Google search. A person in distress, typing the job and the town, choosing whoever answers first. You were right, and it settles what runs in summer.

🔴🔴 But it also explains why Google is the wrong tool in January. Nobody searches for air conditioning in a Texas winter, so there is no demand to capture. Winter demand has to be created, and that is a different platform.

🔴🔴 The recommendation, in one line

Meta in winter to create demand. Google in summer to capture it.

⭐ Meta costs roughly a quarter of Google per click and less than half per lead, which matters most in the months when you can least afford waste. And the winter product, maintenance plans, is exactly what Meta is cheapest at: $40 to $80 a sign-up.

⭐ Then in February the money moves to Google, because by then the demand exists and the job is simply to be the firm that answers.

⚠ Local Services Ads are the cheapest Google route at about $51 a lead, but they are pay-per-lead, and you have been burned by pay-per-lead twice. ⭐ The difference is that Google lets you dispute and reclaim a bad one, which neither of your previous suppliers did. We would not start there, but it is worth revisiting once you trust the numbers.

🔴🔴 The point of winter, which is not winter revenue

⭐⭐ Winter is not when you earn. It is when you fill the summer book.

You take one or two calls a day in January and ten a day in July. ⚠ No advertising budget changes that, and we are not going to pretend otherwise.

🔴 So every winter campaign below has the same job: put customers on your calendar for a season that has not started yet, so that when May arrives you are not starting from zero and competing for emergencies at the dearest cost per lead of the year.

🔴🔴 How each one actually runs, because the format matters as much as the platform

META, in your quiet months. Native forms, with one page behind them.

  • Heating checks and tune-ups go to a form inside Facebook itself. Nobody leaves the app, which is why these are the cheapest leads in the trade. The ad is a photograph of your own truck and one line about a noisy furnace in Graham
  • 🔴🔴 But a one-tap form is exactly how you were burned twice. So we use the higher-intent version, which adds a review step, and we put two qualifying questions in front of it: do you own the property, and what is the system doing. ⭐ That is the practical answer to your question about bogus leads: most of them never finish the form
  • The maintenance plan gets a page of its own, because a plan has to be explained and the explanation is the sale. One page, one offer, the July priority promise at the top

⭐⭐ And this solves the Hibu problem. A page we build and host does not touch your website at all. ⭐ So if Hibu will not give us access, the campaigns still run. Your website gets better if we can reach it, and the advertising works either way.

GOOGLE, in season. Mostly not a form at all.

  • 🔴 Emergency repair should ring your phone, not open a form. Your own words were "can you get here right away? I need you who can be here the fastest." A form is friction on a 110 degree afternoon, so these run as call ads where the click is the phone call
  • Replacements and mini splits go to a page with a form and a number. That is a planned purchase, often looked at in the evening, and those people will type
  • Your own name runs all year on a small budget. ⭐ Around $34 a lead, the cheapest thing available to you. Sixteen years of word of mouth means a lot of people search for you by name, and you do not want a competitor buying that
  • ⚠⚠ We would NOT use Performance Max, Google's automatic campaign type. It is cheaper on paper, but it is a black box, and being able to see where a lead came from is the entire point for you. You have been asked to trust somebody twice already

⭐⭐ The thing that decides whether any of this works is how fast a lead gets answered. A tune-up enquiry that sits for four hours is wasted money. ⭐ You already have Podium answering when you cannot, which is better than most firms your size have, and it should be wired to these campaigns from the first day.

⭐ The quiet-season offers, October to January

⭐⭐ Every one of these is a service call, because your installs already hold steady at two a week all year. The gap is the one or two calls a day, and service calls are exactly what idle, already-paid technicians can take.

OfferWhy it works in your quiet monthsPlatform
1. The maintenance plan. An annual agreement: two visits a year, priority booking, a discount on repairs ⭐⭐ The strongest card you hold, and only a busy firm can play it. In a July heatwave everyone waits three days for a technician. Selling "you go to the front of the queue in July" in November is worth real money to a homeowner, it costs you nothing while the vans are quiet, and it turns your slow months into recurring revenue Meta. $40 to $80 a sign-up
2. The heating check and tune-up. A low-ticket winter visit 🔴🔴 The money is not only the visit, it is what the visit finds. A technician in the house in December is the cheapest way there is to find a system that will not survive the summer, and that customer already trusts you before July Meta. $25 to $55 a form fill, the cheapest lead in the table
3. Commercial maintenance contracts for restaurants, convenience stores and retail with refrigeration ⚠ Texas refrigeration failures peak May to August. A restaurant that loses a walk-in in July loses its stock. Selling that protection in January is easy; selling it in July is too late. Recurring, commercial, and indifferent to today's weather Google, low budget. Business owners search for this
4. Mini splits for garages, additions and home offices ⭐ You are a Trane and Mitsubishi dealer, and this is a planned purchase rather than an emergency. People plan home projects in winter Meta. It sells on the photograph
The in-season campaigns, February to September
  • Google search, emergency intent. ac repair graham tx, air conditioner not working near me, emergency ac repair. ⚠ Dearest leads of the year at around $149 on non-branded search, and worth every cent in July
  • Google search, replacement intent. ac replacement cost, new ac unit graham, mini split installation. Longer consideration, far higher ticket
  • Branded search, all year, tiny budget. ⭐ About $34 a lead, the cheapest thing on this page. It stops a competitor buying your name from someone who was already looking for you. Sixteen years of word of mouth makes this unusually valuable for you
  • Meta retargeting. Anyone who visited and did not call. Cheap, and only possible once the pixel you already own is actually wired up
  • Meta to your maintenance plan list. The people who bought a plan in January are the warmest replacement prospects you have in July
⚠ The order it has to happen in
  • Now, October. Tracking installed, forms on every page, heating and maintenance pages built. Your quiet season has already started, so this cannot wait for spring
  • November to January. Meta on maintenance plans and tune-ups. Filling idle, already-paid technician time, and measuring every call
  • February. Budget begins moving to Google as your season starts
  • May to August. Full weight on Google, emergency and replacement. The commercial refrigeration page is ranking by now, which is the reason to build it in winter
  • September. Review everything against real numbers, which by then you will have for the first time
